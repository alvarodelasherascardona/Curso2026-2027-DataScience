Hands-On Assignment 2: Madrid Urban Benches

Dataset: 300095-0-mobiliario-bancos.csv.

1. Dataset analysis

  • CSV, UTF-8 with BOM, semicolon delimiter.
  • 76,781 records and 22 attributes.
  • ID has 76,781 non-null unique values and is the candidate key.
  • 22 district values and 132 neighbourhood values.
  • Projected X/Y coordinates are present in 76,768 rows; latitude/longitude in 4,060 rows.
  • DESC_CLASIFICACION, ESTADO and SISTEMA_COORD are constant in this snapshot.

Data-quality decisions

  • Codes are strings because they are identifiers, not quantities.
  • Empty cells generate no triple.
  • COD_POSTAL=0 and FECHA_INSTALACION=1900-01-01 require review as possible sentinel values.
  • Coordinates use xsd:decimal. No coordinate transformation will be made until the complete CRS, beyond the supplied ETRS89 label, is confirmed.
  • Street names are lowercased, de-accented and hyphenated only for URI slugs; their original labels are preserved.

3. Resource naming strategy

Proposed ontology namespace: http://bancos.madrid.linkeddata.es/ontology#. This is different from the default Protégé namespace. The group does not claim that these URIs are currently operational; a production deployment must control the domain.

ResourcePatternExample
Class/property.../ontology#{term}.../ontology#Bench
Bench.../resource/bench/{ID}.../resource/bench/100005
Street.../resource/street/{slug}.../resource/street/calle-ezequiel-solana
Neighbourhood.../resource/neighborhood/{COD_BARRIO}.../resource/neighborhood/152
District.../resource/district/{COD_DISTRITO}.../resource/district/15
City.../resource/city/{name}.../resource/city/madrid
  • Classes use UpperCamelCase; properties use lowerCamelCase.
  • Different classes have different individual paths.
  • In production, slash URIs use 303 redirects and content negotiation: text/html, text/turtle, application/ld+json and application/rdf+xml, with Vary: Accept.
